Chapter 1 Platform Legitimacy & Regulation
1.1 Regulatory Status

NIHA Carbon operates in strict compliance with AMLO Cap. 615 (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance), implementing mandatory KYC/AML procedures for all clients prior to onboarding. All records are retained for a minimum of 7 years post-termination. Regarding formal registration with ESMA or EU national financial authorities, specific regulatory certifications are available upon request via the NIHA Compliance Pack. Please contact compliance@nihaogroup.com.

1.2 Authorisation & Operating Basis

NIHA Carbon operates as a trading platform and intermediary, facilitating the exchange of EU Allowances (EUA) and Chinese Emission Allowances (CEA) between counterparties through a structured order book and settlement infrastructure. Spot EUA transactions are currently excluded from MiFID II financial instrument classification under EU ETS regulations. Clients are advised to verify their own regulatory obligations independently.

1.3 Registration & Licensing

Entity incorporation documentation, AML/CTF policy, and applicable registration or licensing details are provided as part of the standard NIHA Compliance Pack, made available to clients upon request during onboarding. The platform maintains a 7-year record retention policy for all KYC, transactional, and STR records in compliance with AMLO Cap. 615.

1.4 Operational History & Volume

Trading volume history and operational statistics are available upon execution of a non-disclosure agreement. Please contact your account representative to request detailed platform performance data.

Chapter 2 Platform Security & Settlement Processes
2.1 EUA Transfer Execution

EUA transfers on NIHA Carbon follow a structured settlement process with full status tracking and registry linkage. Upon execution, settlement progresses through five auditable stages, each timestamped and corresponding to a real-world registry step, culminating in registry-level confirmation of transfer into the client's nominated EU ETS account.

Settlement Status Flow
Stage 1Pending — trade confirmed, settlement initiated
Stage 2Transfer Initiated — registry instruction submitted
Stage 3In Transit — transfer in progress
Stage 4At Custody — certificates held pending final delivery
Stage 5Settled — registry-level confirmation issued
2.2 Internal Ledger & Registry Linkage

The platform maintains an internal ledger to reflect pre-settlement positions during the settlement window, which is reconciled against the official EU Union Registry upon settlement completion. Clients receive direct registry-level confirmation of transfer into their nominated account at the Settled stage.

2.3 KYC, AML & Security Measures

The platform implements a mandatory 5-step KYC process for all clients, comprising: (i) PEP declarations for all directors and UBOs; (ii) carbon market experience and risk appetite assessment; (iii) source of funds declaration and expected transaction volume; (iv) tax residency and CRS disclosures; and (v) 9 mandatory legal declarations. Every platform action generates an immutable audit ticket with action type, entity, timestamp, and transaction reference. All KYC and transactional records are retained for 7 years post-termination per AMLO Cap. 615.

2.4 Standard Settlement Timelines

Settlement timelines are defined at the transaction level and tracked automatically by the platform's settlement processor. Business days are calculated on a Monday–Friday basis, excluding public holidays. Overdue monitoring alerts trigger at +1 business day (warning) and +3 business days (critical) past expected settlement date.

2.5 Platform Insolvency & Technical Failure Protections

Client assets are maintained in segregated entity-level holdings, tracked independently at the entity level with full auditability. All balances and transaction records are persisted in an independently backed-up database infrastructure. The settlement processor operates continuous monitoring with automated escalation for overdue or stuck settlements. Fund return requests are processed within 5 business days per the terms of the NIHA Custody Agreement.

3.2 Benchmark Pricing & Transparency

EUA and CEA prices are sourced in real-time from carboncredits.com and Trading Economics, providing benchmark-linked pricing consistent with EEX and ICE spot market references. The CEA/EUA swap ratio is derived directly as CEA price (EUR) ÷ EUA price (EUR), with full price history accessible to all authenticated users.

3.3 Disclosure of All Costs

All fees applicable to a transaction are disclosed prior to execution. The platform does not charge separate settlement fees, transfer fees, or registry fees. The trading fees stated in Article 3.1 are the sole costs imposed by the platform on client transactions.

Chapter 4 Counterparty & Transaction Risk
4.1 Platform Role — Principal vs. Intermediary

NIHA Carbon acts exclusively as intermediary. The platform facilitates trades through an order book mechanism and does not hold or sell EUA inventory on its own account. Liquidity on the platform is provided by verified Market Maker entities, which serve as counterparties when client orders are matched against their liquidity.

4.2 Counterparty Identification

All counterparties, including Market Maker entities, are subject to the same mandatory KYC/AML onboarding process as buyers. Each entity is individually verified, screened against sanctions lists, and approved by the NIHA compliance team prior to being permitted to trade. Counterparty identity records are retained for 7 years per AMLO Cap. 615.

4.3 Funding Model & Settlement Assurance

The platform operates on a pre-funding model. Client wire transfers are announced in advance, confirmed by the platform, and cleared through AML review before trading access is granted. This mechanism provides equivalent protection to a Delivery vs Payment (DvP) arrangement: client funds are verified and secured prior to any certificate transfer entering the settlement chain.

4.4 Legal Recourse & Governing Law

The governing law, dispute resolution mechanism, and jurisdiction applicable to all platform transactions are specified in the NIHA Platform Agreement executed by each client during onboarding. The platform's compliance framework references AMLO Cap. 615 as a key regulatory baseline. For the current version of the Platform Agreement, contact legal@nihaogroup.com.

Chapter 5 Verification & Audit Trail
5.1 Pre-Purchase Evidence of Holdings

Real-time EUA and CEA holdings are maintained at the entity level and are visible to authenticated users via the platform dashboard. Institutional counterparties requiring formal written evidence of holdings prior to a transaction should contact their account representative, who will arrange the appropriate documentation.

5.2 Registry-Level Transfer Confirmation

Upon settlement completion (status: Settled), clients receive registry-level confirmation of EUA transfer into their nominated EU ETS registry account. The At Custody stage confirms that certificates are held pending final delivery; the Settled stage confirms that the EU Union Registry transfer is complete and the certificates are in the client's account.

5.3 Transaction Audit Trail

Every deposit, trade, settlement, and administrative action executed on the platform generates an immutable audit ticket comprising action type, entity identifier, timestamp, and transaction reference. Settlement status transitions are individually timestamped across all five stages. All KYC documentation and client declarations are stored under a 7-year retention policy per AMLO Cap. 615. Clients may request a full export of their transaction history at any time by contacting compliance@nihaogroup.com.
