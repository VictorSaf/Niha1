01 —— Platform Legitimacy & Regulation
Question

Is the platform regulated by ESMA or national financial authorities?

NIHA Carbon operates in strict compliance with AMLO Cap. 615 (Anti-Money Laundering and Counter-Terrorist Financing Ordinance), implementing mandatory KYC/AML procedures for all clients prior to onboarding. All records are retained for 7 years post-termination. Specific regulatory registration details are available upon request via our Compliance Pack.
Question

Does it operate as an authorised exchange, broker, or intermediary under EU ETS rules?

NIHA Carbon operates as a trading platform and intermediary, facilitating EUA and CEA exchange through a structured order book and settlement infrastructure. Spot EUA transactions are currently excluded from MiFID II financial instrument classification under EU ETS regulations — clients should verify their own regulatory obligations independently.
02 —— Security & Settlement Processes
Question

What security measures are in place — KYC, AML, fraud prevention, transaction monitoring?

The platform implements a 5-step KYC process including PEP declarations for all directors and UBOs, carbon market experience assessment, source of funds declaration, tax residency and CRS disclosures, and 9 mandatory legal declarations. All first deposits are subject to a mandatory 3-day AML hold; large deposits ≥ €500K trigger enhanced due diligence. Every action generates an immutable audit ticket.

Deposits ≥ €500,000 are subject to extended AML review. First-time deposits from new clients are held for 3 business days regardless of amount.
Question

What are the settlement timelines and how are transfers tracked?

Settlement progresses through five auditable stages: Pending → Transfer Initiated → In Transit → At Custody → Settled. Each stage corresponds to a real-world registry step. Overdue alerts trigger at +1 biz day (warning) and +3 biz days (critical).

03 —— Fees & Pricing Transparency
Question

What are the platform's trading fees, and are there any hidden costs?

The fee structure is flat and fully transparent. CEA cash purchases: 1.5% of transaction value. CEA/EUA swaps: 2.0% of transaction value. There are no custody fees, maintenance fees, or withdrawal surcharges. Fund return requests are processed within 5 biz days at no additional cost.
Question

Is pricing transparent and linked to EUA spot benchmarks?

Yes. EUA and CEA prices are sourced in real-time from carboncredits.com and Trading Economics. The CEA/EUA swap ratio is derived directly as CEA price ÷ EUA price — ensuring full benchmark linkage. Price history is accessible to all authenticated users.
04 —— Counterparty & Transaction Risk
Question

Does the platform act as principal or only as intermediary?

NIHA Carbon acts as intermediary only. The platform facilitates trades through an order book mechanism. Liquidity is provided by verified Market Maker entities — which serve as counterparties when trading against their orders. NIHA Carbon does not hold or sell its own EUA inventory.
Question

Does the platform require pre-funding, or does it support DvP settlement?

The platform operates on a pre-funding model: wire transfers are announced in advance, confirmed, and cleared through AML review before trading access is granted. This provides the equivalent protection of a Delivery vs Payment arrangement — funds are verified and secured before certificates are transferred through the settlement chain.
05 —— Verification & Audit Trail
Question

Will you receive registry-level confirmation of transfer into your nominated EU ETS account?

Yes. Upon reaching Settled status, clients receive registry-level confirmation of EUA transfer into their nominated EU ETS account. The AT_CUSTODY stage confirms certificates are held pending final delivery; SETTLED confirms the registry transfer is complete.
Question

Is there a complete audit trail for all transactions carried out on the platform?

Every deposit, trade, settlement, and administrative action generates an immutable audit ticket with action type, entity, timestamp, and reference. Settlement statuses are timestamped at each of the five transition stages. All KYC documents and declarations are stored with a 7-year retention policy per AMLO Cap. 615. Full transaction history is available for export at any time.
