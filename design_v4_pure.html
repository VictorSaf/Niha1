Q&A

Responses to institutional and professional client enquiries regarding platform operations, compliance, and settlement infrastructure.

01 Legitimacy & Regulation

Is the platform regulated by ESMA or national financial authorities?

NIHA Carbon operates in compliance with AMLO Cap. 615, implementing mandatory KYC/AML procedures for all clients prior to onboarding. All records are retained for 7 years post-termination. Regulatory registration details are available upon request via our Compliance Pack.

Does it operate as an authorised exchange, broker, or intermediary?

NIHA Carbon operates as a trading platform and intermediary, facilitating EUA and CEA exchange through a structured order book and settlement infrastructure. Spot EUA transactions are currently excluded from MiFID II financial instrument classification under EU ETS regulations.

How long has the platform been operating, and what is its trading volume history?

Volume figures and operational history are available upon request under NDA. Please contact your account representative for detailed trading statistics.

02 Security & Settlement

How are EUA transfers executed on the platform?

Settlement progresses through five auditable stages: Pending → Transfer Initiated → In Transit → At Custody → Settled. Each stage corresponds to a real-world registry step with timestamped confirmation. Overdue alerts trigger at +1 and +3 business days past expected settlement.

What security measures are in place — KYC, AML, transaction monitoring?

The platform implements a 5-step KYC process including PEP declarations for all directors and UBOs, source of funds declaration, CRS disclosures, and 9 mandatory legal declarations. First deposits are subject to a 3-day AML hold; deposits of €500,000 or more trigger enhanced due diligence. Every action generates an immutable audit ticket.

What are the standard settlement timelines?

CEA cash purchase: T+3 business days. Swap CEA leg: T+2. Swap EUA leg: T+5. Full cycle purchase to EUA delivery: T+10 business days. Fund return requests are processed within 5 business days.

03 Fees & Transparency

What are the platform's trading fees? Are there any hidden costs?

The fee structure is flat and fully transparent. CEA cash purchases: 1.5% of transaction value. CEA/EUA swaps: 2.0% of transaction value. There are no custody fees, maintenance fees, withdrawal surcharges, or registry transfer fees.

Is pricing linked to EUA spot/futures benchmarks?

Yes. EUA and CEA prices are sourced in real-time from carboncredits.com and Trading Economics. The CEA/EUA swap ratio is derived directly as CEA price ÷ EUA price, ensuring full benchmark linkage. Price history is accessible to all authenticated users.

04 Counterparty Risk

Does the platform act as principal or only as intermediary?

NIHA Carbon acts as intermediary only. Liquidity is provided by verified Market Maker entities which serve as counterparties when trading against their orders. NIHA Carbon does not hold or sell its own EUA inventory.

Does the platform require pre-funding, or does it support DvP settlement?

The platform operates on a pre-funding model: client wire transfers are announced in advance, confirmed, and cleared through AML review before trading access is granted. This provides equivalent protection to a Delivery vs Payment arrangement — funds are verified and secured before certificates enter the settlement chain.

What legal recourse is available in case of dispute?

Governing law, dispute resolution mechanism, and jurisdiction are specified in the NIHA Platform Agreement executed during onboarding. Contact legal@nihaogroup.com for the current version.

05 Verification & Audit Trail

Can the platform provide evidence of EUAs held before purchase?

Real-time EUA and CEA holdings are maintained at the entity level and available to authenticated clients via the platform dashboard. For institutional pre-trade proof of holdings, contact your account representative.

Will you receive registry-level confirmation of transfer into your nominated EU ETS account?

Yes. Upon reaching Settled status, clients receive registry-level confirmation of EUA transfer into their nominated EU ETS account. The At Custody stage confirms certificates are held pending final delivery; Settled confirms the registry transfer is complete.

Is there a complete audit trail for all transactions?

Every deposit, trade, settlement, and administrative action generates an immutable audit ticket with action type, entity, timestamp, and reference. All KYC documents and declarations are stored with a 7-year retention policy per AMLO Cap. 615. Full transaction history is available for export at any time.
